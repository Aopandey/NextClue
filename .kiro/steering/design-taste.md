# Design taste (from Mike's Lavish feedback)

`design.md` holds the tokens and bans. This file holds the judgement Mike keeps asking for.

## Less on screen
- Show what needs to be acted on first; put detail behind open/close disclosures. "Too much going on" and "a lot of text everywhere" are the most common complaints.
- Keep graphs to a few readable nodes; if it is hard to see at a glance, it has too many.
- Each box gets a short overview and hyperlinks to the depth, not the full text.
- Plain, low-friction wording: say what is happening and what to do next.

## Trust
- Link evidence to the actual paper or record (PubMed, NCBI Bookshelf), never a dead or placeholder link.
- Hovering or focusing an item shows a short description of it, with a visible outline on the item.
- Check every date shown against its source record before showing a mock.

## Exploring
- Offer real variety: several colour and layout variations, not small tweaks of one.
- Look outside for inspiration (real hospital sites, the web) when asked for new directions.
- Every artifact — app screens, Lavish pages, logo boards — uses the health-screen tokens in `frontend/app/globals.css`.

## Logos only
- Symmetrical and simple; no boxes, no dotted lines. (The constellation's dotted and dashed edges are a spec decision and stay.)
